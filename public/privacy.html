Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. Information We Collect

Spotify Transferrer requires access to your Spotify account to function. During the OAuth flow, we request access to your public profile and your Liked Songs library (`user-library-read`, `user-library-modify`).

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we access is strictly used to facilitate the transfer of your Liked Songs from one account to another. All data processing happens entirely within your local browser session.

3. Data Storage

We do not store or log your personal data. Your Spotify tokens, track IDs, and profile information are stored temporarily in your browser's `sessionStorage` and are cleared immediately once you close the tab or start over.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the Spotify Web API to fetch and insert your songs. Please refer to Spotify's Privacy Policy to understand how they handle your data.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
